    How pure are Nova Lab research peptides?

    Purity is verified at 99% or higher by HPLC, and identity is confirmed against certified reference standards at an independent ISO 17025 accredited US laboratory, alongside content, appearance, fentanyl, heavy metals, sterility, and endotoxin testing on every batch.

    How should research peptides be stored?

    Sealed lyophilized vials are heat-stable and can be kept at room temperature, refrigerated, or frozen; stability doesn't change much as long as the vial stays sealed. Protect from light and moisture, and refrigerate reconstituted solutions at 2-8°C.
