Every batch is 8x tested at independent ISO 17025 accredited laboratories: purity (HPLC), net peptide content, identity, appearance, fentanyl screen, heavy metals (ICP-MS), sterility (PCR), and endotoxin. We publish full Certificates of Analysis for complete transparency.

Our Quality Process

A rigorous six-step process ensures every peptide meets our exacting standards.

01

Raw Material Verification

Our manufacturing partners test all raw materials for identity and purity before synthesis begins, verifying amino acid purity, solvent quality, and reagent specifications.

02

Peptide Synthesis

Partner facilities run solid-phase peptide synthesis (SPPS) in controlled cleanroom environments, following validated protocols with real-time monitoring.

03

Purification

Reverse-phase HPLC purification at the production facility removes impurities and ensures target purity levels. Multiple purification passes when necessary.

04

Independent Identity & Content Testing

Independent ISO 17025 accredited laboratories run an 8-assay panel on every batch we source: purity, net peptide content, identity, appearance, fentanyl screen, heavy metals, sterility, and endotoxin.

05

Documentation

Full Certificate of Analysis generated with all test results. Batch records maintained for complete traceability.

06

Packaging & Storage

Lyophilized peptides sealed in sterile vials under nitrogen. Climate-controlled storage until shipment.
